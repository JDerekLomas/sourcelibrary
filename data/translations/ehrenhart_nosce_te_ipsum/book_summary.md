# Nosce te ipsum (Know Thyself)
## Ernestus Aurelius Reger von Ehrenhart

### Overview

This treatise presents a theosophical and alchemical approach to medicine, arguing that true healing knowledge comes from divine illumination and understanding the correspondence between the microcosm (man) and macrocosm (universe). The text synthesizes Paracelsian medicine, Christian mysticism, and Hermetic philosophy into a unified theory of the "Universal Medicine."

### Structure

The work progresses through several interconnected themes:

1. **Divine Origin of Medicine** (Pages 1-4): True medicine cannot be invented by man but must be revealed by God through the Holy Spirit. The medicine of the body prefigures the medicine of the soul.

2. **Self-Knowledge as Foundation** (Pages 4-6): The ancient injunction "Nosce te ipsum" (Know thyself) is presented as the key to medical understanding. By studying one's own body, one learns the principles governing all of nature.

3. **The Microcosm-Macrocosm Harmony** (Pages 6-8): Detailed exposition of how the human body mirrors cosmic processes. The stomach's separation of nutrients parallels the Last Judgment's separation of good from evil.

4. **The Two Central Fires** (Pages 8-10): The heart's fire becomes light in the head/brain. Understanding these "two central fires" (macro- and microcosmic) is the key to all disease and medicine.

5. **Mumia and Magnetic Action** (Pages 10-15): The concept of "Mumia" - a vital magnetic essence in all things - explains action at a distance, sympathetic healing, and the connection between separated parts. The sun and air serve as universal instruments of fermentation.

### Major Themes

1. **Divine Source of Knowledge**: Only God can create a true physician. Medical knowledge requires divine illumination, not just academic study.

2. **The One Universal Medicine**: A single medicine exists for all illnesses, accessible through understanding the unity underlying all phenomena.

3. **Microcosm-Macrocosm Correspondence**: Man is a "little world" containing all the principles of the greater universe. What operates in nature operates in man.

4. **Mumia as Vital Essence**: A magnetic, breathable life-force connects all things. This "Mumia" explains sympathetic magic, action at a distance, and the connection between separated body parts.

5. **Fire and Light Transformation**: The heart's fire becomes the brain's light. This transformation is key to understanding both physiology and spiritual development.

6. **Solve et Coagula**: The alchemical principle of dissolution and coagulation governs all natural processes, from digestion to cosmic movements.

### Historical Significance

This text exemplifies the tradition of spiritual alchemy and theosophical medicine that flourished in early modern Germany. It draws on:

- **Paracelsian Medicine**: The concept of the microcosm-macrocosm, Mumia, and spiritual essences
- **Jacob Böhme's Theosophy**: The emphasis on divine illumination and inner spiritual processes
- **Hermetic Philosophy**: The correspondence between above and below, the One in All
- **Christian Mysticism**: The integration of alchemical concepts with biblical imagery (Last Judgment, Christ as Judge)
