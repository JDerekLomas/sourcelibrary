# Glossary

Key terms from *Nosce te ipsum* by Ernestus Aurelius Reger von Ehrenhart.

---

## A

**Actio in distans** (Action at a distance) — pp. 8, 9, 10
The phenomenon of influencing an object without physical contact, explained through the magnetic Mumia and cosmic forces.

**Anatomia viva** (Living anatomy) — p. 6
A method of extracting the essence from a living person's stomach without causing harm.

**Ars artium, Scientia scientiarum** (Art of arts, Science of sciences) — p. 2
A description of the profound knowledge of divine medicine, possessed by the ancient Magi.

## B

**Balsamus sanguinis** (Balsam of the blood) — p. 8
The essential, healing principle within blood that can be released through spiritual fermentation and returned to the body.

## C

**Chymia vera** (True alchemy) — p. 6
The authentic alchemical knowledge that "Sophists" cannot grasp, distinguished from false or superficial alchemy.

**Cura magnetica** (Magnetic cure) — p. 8
Healing at a distance through the magnetic properties of the Mumia.

## E

**Essentiam** (Essence) — pp. 5, 6
The vital substance or spirit of a thing, particularly of the stomach.

## F

**Fermentation** — pp. 8, 9, 10
The spiritual process by which vital essence is activated or "leavened," allowing it to be released and reconnected with its source.

## H

**Humidum radicale** (Radical moisture) — pp. 9, 10
An alchemical concept of fundamental, life-sustaining moisture, identified with air and originating from the "cold central fire."

## I

**Ignis centralis** (Central fire) — pp. 6, 8, 10
The fundamental fire at the center of all things; exists as both hot (sun) and cold (moon) forms.

**Instrumentum universale igneum** (Universal instrument of fire) — pp. 9, 10
The sun, described as the primary mover that activates and ripens all things.

## M

**Macrocosm** — passim
The universe or greater world, which corresponds to the microcosm (man).

**Medicamenta** (Medicines) — p. 6
Medicines that can be prepared either from the microcosm (man) or the macrocosm (universe).

**Microcosmus** (Microcosm) — passim
Man as a miniature representation of the universe, containing all cosmic principles within himself.

**Mumia** — pp. 8, 9, 10, 11
The vital magnetic essence or life-force in all things. In humans, it is "triplex" (threefold) and enables action at a distance and sympathetic healing.

**Mumia corporis** (Mummy of the body) — p. 9
The vital substance or "balsam" homogenous with the human body, containing magnetic sympathy and antipathy.

## N

**Nosce te ipsum** (Know thyself) — pp. 1, 2, 4
The ancient Greek injunction (from the Temple of Apollo at Delphi) presented as the foundational principle for medical knowledge.

## P

**Polus Arcticus / Antarcticus** (Arctic/Antarctic Pole) — p. 10
The magnetic poles referenced in the context of the vital breath's circulation.

**Prima materia spermatica** (Spermatic primary matter) — p. 4
The single, original substance from which all parts of the human body originate.

## S

**Solve et Coagula** (Dissolve and Coagulate) — p. 6
The fundamental alchemical processes of breaking down and re-forming, said to be caused by the "two central fires."

**Spiritus** (Spirit) — passim
The subtle, vital principle that mediates between soul and body; exists in natural, vital, and animal forms.

**Spiritus Mumiae vivae** (Spirit of the living Mumia) — p. 8
The living breath of the Mumia, full of fiery spirit of life.

**Sympathia / Antipathia** (Sympathy / Antipathy) — pp. 8, 9
The attractive and repulsive forces that connect or repel things, depending on their nature.

## T

**Transplantatio** (Transplantation) — p. 9
The magical transference of diseases or vital force from one being to another.

## V

**Vim attractivam** (Attractive force) — p. 8
The magnetic-like pull that a severed part retains, drawing the "breath" of the main body to itself.

---

## Concepts

**Two Central Fires**: The macro- and microcosmic fires that govern all qualities and processes (Heat/Cold, Solve/Coagula).

**Divine Origin of Medicine**: Only God can create a true physician; medical knowledge requires divine illumination.

**One Universal Medicine**: A single medicine exists for all illnesses, accessible through understanding unity.

**Fire-Light Transformation**: The heart's fire transforms into the brain's light, governing understanding and the senses.

**Spiritual Fermentation**: The process by which vital essence is spiritually activated, allowing it to be released and returned to its source.

---

*Note: Many terms appear in Germanized Latin spelling (e.g., "primä" for "prima"). The author freely mixes German prose with Latin technical vocabulary, characteristic of early modern theosophical and alchemical texts.*
