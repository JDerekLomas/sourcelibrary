Thus all action depends only on the preparation of this medicine / on comprehending / how everything is made from one / and is maintained from one / and in all individual parts through the One the good is separated from the being in its degrees / so that it may also be understood again through Art / when it is separated from the temperature. So that in the Subjecto medicinæ quocunque the One / in which all lies in all / is opened through the true preparation / and not closed by the false one / and thus is hindered in its effect.

How this is to be done / mark the following in yourself: The stomach is the universal preparer of the Chyli nutriticii for all members through its Reductionem radicalem into materiam primam viscosam per materiam primam, whereby the universal Art omnium formarum capax is all the better opened into the essence. The universal nutriment prepared by the healthy stomach / is then further specified by each member's singular Archæus, as the place's particularly predominating source-spirit. But you, learn to recognize this Universal-Salis Instrumentum univerfale & Eitentiale igneum / then you will find the Omnia in omnibus quite easily. But if you do not know yourself / and what you have on and in you / without which you cannot live or be healthy / then you have truly not entered this Art through the right door and you seek in vain outside of yourself / its same and uniform Agens, through which you must prepare the true medicines only in their own stomach-fire. Praised be the Almighty and All-merciful Good / who has hidden this despised and lowly thing from the prudent and wise of the world from the beginning until now / and has revealed it only to His humble and immature ones. Who then would want to break this seal against God's will / and reveal such secrets / of which God himself must be the master? and even if one were to reveal it / the world would still rather despise / than believe / and accept the same.

The Cacodæmonic Anatomists / to learn this secret in man / have brought many innocent ones to their death and murdered them through their fine thinking; often also sought in the dead / that which is to be found only in the living and healthy. Therefore no one will recognize this secret / unless he wishes / what Anatomia corporis vivi may be / the body and all its parts uninjured / of which Paracelsus says: that one can draw the essence of a member / from the member without harm / and thereby perform all wonders. This Anatomy teaches one to know the human body and its parts much better and more thoroughly / than all the flaying and cutting of dead or even living bodies. Many have indeed dared / to write of this Muniä essentiali vivâ; but none yet to my knowledge has hit the right mark. The Adepti have revealed this Art under an artful cloak as their greatest secret only to the dignis / because after its recognition and preparation everything else is but a child's play / leaving it thus to God / to send the advocates of the immutable and immutable life.
