And to give you cause for further reflection / I will very briefly describe the functions of some of the foremost members of the human body / from which one can sufficiently recognize the true preparation of the Universal-medicine.

The Teeth are the grinding stone / by which the coarse material is made small.

The Stomach is the vessel and the oven / in which and by which the essential fire digests, putrefies, solves, and cooks its salt-spirit to the prima materia viscosa / that is / makes a Chylus from the food: it gives the coarse salt to the bladder / the sulphuric feces to the intestines / the superfluous mercurial part to the mouth and other emunctories.

The Vena lactea are the filter, which separate the Chylus, as the nutriment of all members, from the coarsest feces.

The Lungs make all Principia or Elementa into air / in them the fixed becomes volatile / they draw to themselves and give forth again.

The Liver prepares the materia chylosa, to receive the sulphuric color.

The Heart gives it the complete redness / and in the chylous tinder strikes the animal life-tinder / and exalts and fixes everything in the highest degree in the animal manner / whereby the fiery life-solutes in the Humido radicali become blood / which is a universal-microcosmic fiery-watery nurturing Heaven-Dew / resembling in its temperature an Auro potabili, which through its essential moist warmth maintains the whole body in its essence and temperature / so that the true essence of all members lies solely in the water / wherein the fire dwells / and which it moves. This Heaven-Dew transmutes itself into the forma specifica of each member: From it are made in the Renibus the two central spermatic seed-fires by the animal Archæus and artist.
