The fire dwells in the heart; but in the head
and brain it becomes light / which then gives in the
organs the spirit of the senses / and the fire is the light's
sharpness. Now, the more fire in the heaven becomes
light / the sharper and more penetrating is the
understanding. Now, what kind of fire burns in and out of
the heart / of such a quality will
the light also be in the microcosmic heaven of the
head / this then influences from the heaven of the
head as a star into the lower / as into the or-
gans of the senses / and according to this influx
the senses then become through the limbs or organs, where-
from one can discern / whether the fire / light and spirit
of man be good or evil.

Behold / let this be enough of the Harmony
macro- & microcosmica triunä acque catholicä.
He who learns nothing from this / will likely remain in
confusion and uncertainty.

And yet everything lies only in the fundamental
knowledge of the two central fires / both macro- as
well as microcosmic; for these are the cause of all
qualities with their Solve & Coagula, Heat
and Cold. These, being rightly known / give uni-
versally & particularly of all diseases and
also of true medicine a fundamental knowledge.

For the preparation of the true medicine there are no
more than two ways.

The first / that one per Anatomiam vivam
extracts the Essentiam of the stomach / the stomach and
man uninjured / (which can indeed very well
be done by a true artist ) and there-
by prepare the medicines. And thus man has
enough in his own fundamental knowledge.

The other way is / that one learns to prepare
the image of this microcosmic essence in the Macrocosm
/ and thereby justify the Medicamenta.

Both

Both ways are right for the knowing. And the one is not
thoroughly known / unless one also knows
the other. From this it also appears clearly /
what Chymia vera or Arg. chymia may be. For
the Sophists cannot pass these cliffs / but
will certainly be shattered thereon.

Here there would now be much to report / namely how this
knowledge directs us to God and Christ; but
we will pass over it for the sake of worldly matters. But
I give you this to ponder / whether man does not
have a prefiguration in himself of the separation
day of the Last Judgment / when the goats from the
sheep shall be separated by the Judge Christ.
For as his stomach separates the good /
that it is accepted as the essence of all limbs /
but rejects the evil: So also on that
day shall the light be separated from the darkness.
Should we then further report / how Christ described this
day of separation / namely who the reapers
are to be; and then further see in the Scripture /
how their form is described / and bring the same with the
Art into a harmony / then one would have to lay
quite bare / what God still wishes to have hidden.
Let us therefore leave it be / so that the seek-
er may have cause / to ask God for his spirit of
wisdom / who alone must teach him to recognize the
Creator from the creature. To Him alone be
all honor in time and eternity.

I think it thus to be sufficiently proven / that a Uni-
versal Medicine is in nature. But he who his
own experience does not believe / him also all
testimonies do not help. So, beloved friend,
be content with this little / and further do not
doubt the certainty of this Art. Yet it is
certain / that what is known is not so soon worked out /
since it is no small gift of God. Echtles-
