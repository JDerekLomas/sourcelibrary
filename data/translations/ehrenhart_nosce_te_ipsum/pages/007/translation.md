Syr. 4, 12, 13, 14. sends with the saying, speaks: Wisdom raises up her children / and receives those / who seek her. Whoever loves her / loves life: and whoever seeks her diligently / will have great joy. Whoever holds fast to her / will attain great honor: and whatever he undertakes / God will grant success therein.

I should also be able to prove to you quite easily in this treatise / that all diverse operationes medicinarum particularium are brought about by only a single force; indeed, that all operations appearing diverse can be made manifest in every Subjecto at the same time / and must be brought to one Centrum / if a matter is to have the frame of a true Medicine. But because the Paradoxum has already been elaborated elsewhere / the haste / to answer you / has not permitted the digression now.

That you write / that I should write to you the true foundation of Medicină diatatică / because no author has yet contented you on this. So know / that for many important reasons I would have soon kept silent about it; but so that you may nevertheless see my favorable disposition toward you / I will communicate the following to you / which I hope / you will not esteem lightly; but more can follow oretenus / and also be demonstrated practice: although I could show you many other things not only curious, but also in truth useful / about which I find it not at all advisable to report in letters.

So now mark well / God has imparted to all things in nature an attracting / and also a repelling force / in the manner of the magnet / which is their breathing / and thus may very rightly be called their Polus Arcticus and Antarcticus / which breathing is also their true life. Thus all things draw the hidden life / which

which is a spiritual, fiery light inverted into water / in the form of air / just as man draws his breath / to themselves / and give it off again with the outer air / just like man. So it is now certain / that with the common air more than it alone is attracted. And because very wondrous things can be performed not only in Medicine, but also in other arts through this magnetic breathing / so I will reveal to you (yet very cautiously / and yet also clearly enough for one who understands / as I have already explained it sufficiently elsewhere) / what it is / that in all creatures draws this breath to itself and gives it off again / and also show besides / what this breath and its essence may be.

So this breathing now lies in the Mumia of all things / which is a spiritual body and a corporeal spirit. This has the nature of the magnet / that it always draws again toward its origin and place / from which it was broken off: for the origin has within it the true life together with the place of the spirit / namely wherein and whereon the spirit arose / and from which it broke forth: Or / as long as it lives / or has this place of the spiritual Principii vitae within it / which is the mother of the spirit / it draws that which is of its Principii / to itself through its breathing / whatever has been separated from it. (Mark well / the mother of each Principii draws only its own spirit / and longs for it / namely the temporal draws the temporal / but the eternal only the eternal. We are dealing here with the temporal / and yet an application can be made from time to eternity.)

Now it is certain / that in the coarse, heavy body there is no actio / but only in the spiritual one / which is the invisible yet effective force of all things: thus life always draws its like to itself /
