That the vitriol has in itself two kinds of natural sulfur / one metallic and also one vegetable and combustible / which embalmed its salt / all of which are a peculiar Balsam, homogenous to the human Mumia corporis : in addition it is sharpened in its action by the sun / as its rays are introduced into it into a Particularity / having become an awakening life / thereby also made fit / so that the sun / as an Instrumentum universale igneum, which moves everything into actum and into ripening / becomes a medium for this swift action in distans, for it both draws its breath to itself / and with it all the forces / which it has released in all things / and also lets it go from itself again, impregnated with these forces / and thus imparts to each its own ; but the air / which is the rarefied Humidum radicale of the cold central fire / is imbued with the lovely-hot sun-fire / as with the gentle balsamic light rays. For as the sun is the Instrumentum universale igneum caloris innati : so the air is the Instrumentum humid radicalis, which two together in their temperature are the sole cause of the true Fermentation in all things ; while yet the sun in all these remains the Primus and principalis Motor universally / so that all actio in distans throughout the whole world, universality &amp; particulariter, is performed by these two and their right application and their Magnet : for the sun's fiery rays of light take the Humidum

dum radicale of the air unto themselves as a body. When it may be sufficiently proven / what the true Mumia is / in which alone lies the magnetic Sympathia and Antipathia of all things : for according to which of these two is awakened in it / so it acts ; but it performs the great miracles / when it is freed from its fetters of darkness. Now, the more skillfully one knows how to handle it / and then will know how to use it through the application of the Instrumenti universalis calidi &amp; humid / the greater miracles he will perform with it and through it.

Thus one can thereby perform not only these your / but also many more wondrous things / not to be mentioned here / indeed, all Transplantations also proceed from this principle.

And as the sun here / as the hot fire / is the Principal-Cause of the action in distans : So too can strange things be performed by the moon / as the cold fire / likewise also by the stars / because it is evident / that many things in nature regulate themselves according to the moon, indeed, through its appearance they putrefy, become tender / or even vanish entirely. Thus there are also Mumia of the sick and the dead / through which wondrous things can be performed for good as well as for evil ; but because of the misuse, their true principle must be kept secret.

This Universal-Magnet becomes not only in the limbs of man / but also in the different parts of every Creature a special Spiraculum vitæ temporalis, which Spiraculum is like the human Imagination, which can act in distans / through the Medium Universale, which is equally near to all things / and without which nothing can exist / which Imagination produces its effect even over many thousands of miles.
