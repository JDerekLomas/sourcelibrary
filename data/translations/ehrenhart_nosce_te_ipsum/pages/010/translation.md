That now in all things there is such a mumial magnetic breath-taking / which makes an action at a distance / is clearly proven to us by the Vegetabilia, such as tree-fruits / and especially the grapevine. As soon as the sun through its rays awakens its magnetic power / that it gives off its breath and draws it to itself / and thus its Polus Arcticus and Antarcticus begins to operate, so it causes through its exhalation / which the wine that came from it draws to itself / over a long way the wine kept in the cellar and barrel to ferment, and where this is not prevented by art / the Mumia of the wine becomes loose and spiritual / and goes back with the drawing-in of the breath of the grapevine and the sun into its living stalk / and the wine in the barrel spoils. Consider now / whether this does not also happen in all fruit / such as apples / pears / &c. Here one also finds / why most herbs / which are not green in summer and winter / or are of a completely aromatic fiery nature / lose all their powers / so / that one must gather fresh ones every year. For when the roots and trees or the seeds through the power of the sun / as the Instrumentum universale balsamicum &amp; mumiale igneum begin to give off and draw to themselves their breath in the Humido radicali terrae aereo / one finds clearly / that these fruits lose their best scent, color, and taste.

(Here it might well be mentioned in few words / why Harpocrates commands to lay the finger on the mouth) Now in the Medicinis confortantibus and edible things both the spiritual and also fixed Mumia are good by nature ; but in the poisonous things not so / for in them the volatile one / before it is matured by art / is evil by nature and only the fixed is good / and the fixed is vis attractiva, which also draws its poison to itself / and must be made into a fixed medicine, yet not without special manipulations. And the more volatile the poison / the worse it is / for all poison is a volatile / deadly / unripe / congealing cold or an all-gnawing heat ; But the fixed Mumia, from which the poison has departed / is a living and life-giving fixed warmth / which / when it is brought into its Arcanum / can also mature its unripe volatile poison / and through the right art fixate it into a wonder-working medicine. How to elaborate it to its highest degree / it is not necessary to mention. But that Mumia fixa is the progenitor of the volatile poison / I will prove through two Experimenta out of love for my neighbor. Firstly / if someone were given Arsenicum or a metallic poison / then give him Arfenicum cum Nitro fixatum, mixed with Theriac / keep him warm / he will get better with a few doses by the grace of God. It is also good in the pestilence / which has taken its origin from Mineralibus.
