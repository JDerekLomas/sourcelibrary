From all this one can now sufficiently prove also the *Influxum superiorum in inferiora* together with the *Action in diitans*. That thus every creature has its mouth or pores, to release its breath / and to draw it in. And the art consists solely in this / that one correctly opens this mouth for all things / so that they become eager for and agreeable to one another. The mouth of the stars is always open and every moment the stars release a special breath of power from themselves / and also draw the same back to themselves from the lower things; But the mouth of the lower creatures is for the most part closed / to drawing the pure and good stellar breath to themselves. Therefore, it depends only on the right key / that opens and closes / then the art is already found / how one should impregnate and unite the inferior with the upper powers / to do great wonders thereby. From this one also sees / how the *Amuleta, Periapta, Xenextra* work / and how such must be constituted / if they are to bring forth fruit / that thus all *Pori* are such a mouth / through which Nature breathes not only in man / but also in all things / which breathing, in that which draws the mixed breath to itself through art / causes a fermentation / through which the separation of the good from the evil occurs / through which thus the *cura magnetica sympathetica & antipathetica* is performed.

But the *Actio in diitans* in Nature can with nothing

nothing better be compared / than with the human imagination, which works near and far / yes, whereby man's heart itself is near and far to all things / and takes its origin from the two central fires acting upon one another. How the breath of the spirit becomes corporeal and the breath of the elements becomes spiritual in the stars / note the following: The stars give a spirit below them / and the lower world gives a vapor above itself / these come together under the moon: if the conjunction happens near the moon / then the lower becomes spiritual in the upper / and the stars draw one with the other to themselves; but if this conjunction happens near the Earth / then the upper becomes corporeal in and with the lower / and falls down / as can be seen with rain / when the clouds come near to the Earth. And thus the powers of the stars become visible and elemental in this world: If now the lower vapors are pure / there are also pure Gamahcos. With this I shall conclude / and save the rest until our meeting / and in the meantime send you the present [writing] to reflect upon this.
