(24)
Paradox.

Learning / Doctrina, is not Wisdom / Sapientia. For learning is a donum naturale, and is learned from men through man's diligence and skill; But Sapientia is a donum Dei, and cannot be obtained through human diligence and skill. The difference between them is seen in the learned Pharisees and scribal Sadducees / of the Old as well as the New Testament / from whom all mischief and discord have ever come / who also always resisted the Prophets and Apostles / as the Wise. For when learning has violently set itself up in the place of Wisdom / all love and certainty among men has been extinguished / and Hypocrisy and Quarreling together with Discord have ascended the throne / as daily experience shows. But when Wisdom re-enters its dominion / then love and certainty in art and sciences will advance with the Church of God. For this wretched hypocrisy to end / is my best and only wish / and may God grant it out of grace.

E N D
